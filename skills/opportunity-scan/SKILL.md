---
name: opportunity-scan
description: Scan a sector, skill, or business for overlooked opportunities. Use when the user asks for untapped opportunities, gaps in a market, or ways to make money from something.
---

1. Establish the target (sector, skill, or business) and the user's position: skills, resources, constraints. Ask in one message only for what's missing.
2. Find up to 10 overlooked opportunities, ranked by how practical they are for this user. Fewer strong ones beat a padded list.
3. For each opportunity give:
   - what it is
   - why it exists and why most people miss it
   - who would pay, and roughly how much
   - a first step the user could take within 30 days
   - **evidence**: a cited source or concrete signal, or the label "speculation"
4. Search the web when available to back market claims with current sources.

Done when every opportunity carries either evidence or the speculation label, and every first step fits the user's stated position.
