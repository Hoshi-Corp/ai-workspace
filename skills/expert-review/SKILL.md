---
name: expert-review
description: Expert-level review of how the user currently approaches a field, craft, or practice. Use when the user asks how a professional would judge their approach, what beginners get wrong, or how to think at a more advanced level.
---

Review the user's approach the way a demanding senior practitioner in that field would.

1. Establish the field, the user's current approach, their constraints, and their level. Take what you can from the conversation; ask only for what's missing, in one message, then wait.
2. Evaluate the approach: what works, what doesn't, and why — tied to what the user actually described.
3. List the most common beginner mistakes in this field and mark which ones the user is making, with the evidence.
4. Explain what experienced professionals do differently and why it works.
5. Show the expert's thinking: the questions they ask, the trade-offs they weigh, the signals they watch.
6. End with the single highest-leverage change.

Done when every point in steps 2–3 cites something in the user's own approach, not generic advice.
