# expert-review

Get your current approach reviewed by a demanding expert. Claude skill: `skills/expert-review`. The more you say about your approach, constraints, and level, the better the review.

```
Review my approach to [FIELD/SUBJECT] as an experienced practitioner would.
My current approach: [WHAT I DO TODAY]
My constraints and level: [TIME, BUDGET, EXPERIENCE]

1. Evaluate my approach: what's working, what isn't, and why.
2. Name the most common beginner mistakes in this field, and say which ones I'm making.
3. Explain what experienced professionals do differently, and why it works.
4. Show me how an expert thinks about this: the questions they ask and the trade-offs they weigh.
End with the single change that would improve my results the most.
```
