# opportunity-scan

Find overlooked opportunities. Claude skill: `skills/opportunity-scan`. Models invent market facts easily here — the evidence rule is the important part.

```
Analyze [SECTOR, SKILL, OR BUSINESS].
My position: [MY SKILLS, RESOURCES, CONSTRAINTS]

Find up to 10 opportunities most people overlook, ranked by how practical they are for me. For each one:
- what it is
- why it exists and why others miss it
- who would pay for it, and roughly how much
- how I could act on it in the next 30 days
- evidence: cite a source or a concrete signal, or label it "speculation"
Only include opportunities you can justify; fewer good ones beat ten padded ones.
```
