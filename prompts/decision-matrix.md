# decision-matrix

Choose between two options. Claude skill: `skills/decision-matrix`.

```
I'm undecided between [OPTION A] and [OPTION B].
My priorities, most important first: [PRIORITIES — or ask me for them and how much each matters]

Build a decision matrix that compares both options on:
- each of my priorities (weighted by importance)
- risks
- opportunity costs
- long-term consequences
Show the matrix as a table with scores and brief reasons.
Then recommend the option most likely to give the best outcome, say how confident you are, and tell me what would have to be true for the other option to win.
```
