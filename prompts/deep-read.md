# deep-read

Get more than a summary out of something you're reading. Claude skill: `skills/deep-read`.

```
I'll give you a [ARTICLE, BOOK, TRANSCRIPT, OR DOCUMENT]. Go beyond summarizing it. Extract:
1. The most valuable ideas, ranked.
2. The hypotheses it assumes without stating them.
3. Counterintuitive insights: where it contradicts the usual view.
4. Practical applications.
5. What I can apply this week, as concrete actions.
Also say where you think the source is weak, wrong, or overstated.
Quote or point to the passage behind each point.

[PASTE OR ATTACH THE CONTENT]
```
