# explain-two-levels

A one-shot reference card for a topic. For actually learning it, Claude's `learn` skill (diagnose, then teach step by step) usually works better.

```
Explain [TOPIC] to me twice:
1. As if I were 12 years old.
2. At an expert level, including the nuances and the parts experts disagree on.
Then give me the 3 key ideas to remember and one simple analogy that ties them together.
```
